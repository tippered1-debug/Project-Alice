# Economy

The canonical economy links concrete people and institutions to money, labor, goods, and production:

```text
person / institution / firm
  -> owned account and authority
  -> concrete work, payment, order, or obligation
  -> physical inventory and routed delivery
  -> production, consumption, or public service
  -> one-way statistics and UI projections
```

Exact workers hold concrete contracts; payroll settles to their person-owned accounts. Household needs create bids only when backed by canonical balances. A fill transfers money to the real seller and physical stock to the buyer; remote stock remains at origin until freight arrives. Production consumes delivered inventory and emits physical output. Canonical records and transactions own these outcomes.

Legacy population, market, factory, and budget fields remain authored inputs where the scenario importer explicitly uses them, or read projections for existing consumers. They do not provide a second causal economy after the relevant canonical domain is active. The household consumer hard cut and its compatibility boundary are described in the [canonical runtime contract](../migrations/canonical-runtime.md); its completed audit is in the [archive](../archive/README.md).

Construction is an industry. Sponsors fund project accounts and buy materials into isolated yards. Contractors' construction works build with exact workers on ordinary payroll and with equipment they hold. A day of work installs materials in proportion to the work and is paid at a contracted unit price. Completion requires every unit of work and every material. Legacy construction demand and purchased goods are UI projections; see [Canonical construction](../migrations/canonical-construction.md).
