# Government

Each country has a public institution with canonical treasury state. Public activity uses account-backed tax obligations, payroll, procurement, and services. A government capital owner resolves to that institution; public assets and firm equity declare government ownership explicitly where applicable.

National and provincial budget fields are views or policy inputs for existing consumers. They do not create canonical cash or substitute for institutional accounts and transactions. Government staffing, purchasing, and transfers therefore require the responsible institution, account, authority, and any physical goods involved.
